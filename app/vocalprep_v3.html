Help – VocalPrep

1 Capture — take regions out of a lesson recording.

  1. Lessons… › Open recording… and pick the lesson (Dropbox, Downloads…).
  2. ▶ Play and tap + Marker (or M) at each exercise, even while it plays. Type a note for each marker in the list below.
  3. Drag across the waveform to choose a region, or double-tap between two markers. Drag the region's edges to adjust it.
  4. Add to repository →. It is named after its marker. Repeat for every exercise.
  5. Finish lesson removes the full recording from the app (your regions stay) and goes to Practise.

2 Practise — today's list.

  1. Drag regions from the Repository (by ⋮⋮) into the list, or tap +. Drag ⋮⋮ in the list to reorder.
  2. Each region has one note that goes with it every day. Type in the box beside it.
  3. In the small waveform, drag the thin handle to where the singing starts. Then Instructions plays the talking and Sing along starts at the singing. ⟲ loops.
  4. The chips at the top show earlier days, so you can see what you used.
  5. Export ▾ shares the regions (WAV) or your notes, e.g. to Notes or Files.

Keyboard (E2 to G4): choose what a key plays. Note sounds while you hold the key. 5 notes up & down plays the note, then the major scale up to the fifth and back down, at the Tempo set.

Backup ▾ (Repository): Back up to Dropbox… saves every region (WAV) and one file with your names, notes and day lists: choose Save to Files › Dropbox › a folder. Restore from a backup…: pick the .json and the .wav files in that folder (Select › Select All).

Pinch to zoom the lesson waveform, two fingers to scroll, or drag along the strip at the top. Keys: Space play/stop · M marker · L loop · Return add the region · Esc clear.
